Our Story

Years in the making

From school corridors to forever

It began the way the best stories do — quietly, and years before either of them realised. Somewhere between shared classrooms and stolen glances, a friendship took root that neither of them could name yet.

Life pulled them in different directions, across cities and seasons. But some connections don't fade; they simply wait. When their paths crossed again, everything they had once felt returned — softer, surer, and impossible to ignore.

What started as young affection grew into something steady and deep — the kind you build a life around. And so, after all the waiting and the wandering, they chose each other, for always.

…and just like that, we found the one we love
